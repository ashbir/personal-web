I like chips: designing them, simulating them, and seeing them work. By trade
I'm a digital circuit design engineer, so I write RTL for a living. In my free
time I play with analog, mixed-signal and RF circuits, mostly with open-source
tools. I'd love to see chip design become as open as software.

## What I'm into

**Digital design.** My day job. RTL in Verilog, timing, and making logic behave
on silicon.

**Analog, mixed-signal and RF.** My hobby. Schematics, simulation, and modeling,
from transistors up to the system level.

**Open-source IC design.** Open PDKs and open tools, from modeling to layout.

## Background

Before my current job, I spent several years as a research assistant at Okada
Lab, Institute of Science Tokyo (formerly Tokyo Institute of Technology). Along
the way I was a visiting researcher at the University of Michigan for about half
a year. Before coming to Japan, I did layout at Marvell and research at Institut
Teknologi Bandung in Indonesia.
